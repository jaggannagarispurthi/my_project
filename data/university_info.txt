2. VISION

The institution aims to provide quality education and empower women students with technical knowledge, skills and values to become competent and responsible professionals.

3. MISSION

The college focuses on providing quality technical education, developing professional skills, encouraging innovation and research, and creating an environment that supports the academic and overall development of women students.

4. DEPARTMENTS

The college has departments and programmes in areas including:

* Computer Science and Engineering
* Computer Science and Engineering – Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
* Computer Science and Engineering – Data Science
* Computer Science and Engineering – Cyber Security
* Computer Science and Engineering – Internet of Things
* Information Technology
* Electronics and Communication Engineering
* Electrical and Electronics Engineering
* Humanities and Sciences
* Master of Business Administration

5. UNDERGRADUATE COURSES

The college offers the following B.Tech programmes:

* Computer Science and Engineering
* Computer Science and Engineering – Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
* Computer Science and Engineering – Data Science
* Computer Science and Engineering – Cyber Security
* Information Technology
* Electronics and Communication Engineering
* Electrical and Electronics Engineering

6. POSTGRADUATE COURSES

The postgraduate programmes include:

* M.Tech – Computer Science and Engineering
* M.Tech – Embedded Systems
* Master of Business Administration (MBA)

7. COMPUTER SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING

The Computer Science and Engineering programme focuses on computer programming, software development, algorithms, databases, computer networks, artificial intelligence and other areas of computing.

8. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND MACHINE LEARNING

The CSE – Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning programme focuses on artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analysis, intelligent systems and modern computational technologies.

9. DATA SCIENCE

The CSE – Data Science programme focuses on data analysis, statistics, machine learning, data processing and technologies used for extracting useful information from data.

10. CYBER SECURITY

The CSE – Cyber Security programme focuses on cybersecurity, network security, information protection, ethical security practices and protection of computer systems.

11. INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY

The Information Technology programme focuses on software technologies, information systems, databases, networking and application development.

12. ELECTRONICS AND COMMUNICATION ENGINEERING

The Electronics and Communication Engineering programme focuses on electronics, communication systems, digital systems, embedded systems and related technologies.

13. ELECTRICAL AND ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

The Electrical and Electronics Engineering programme covers electrical systems, electronics, electrical machines, power systems and related engineering technologies.
